Source: OpenStreetMap via the public Overpass interpreters.
Snapshot date: see retrievedAt in aguas-lindas-offline-map.json next to this notice.
Query:
[out:json][timeout:120];way[highway](-15.82,-48.35,-15.67,-48.2)->.roads;(.roads;node(w.roads);rel(bw.roads)[type=restriction];);out body;

Each road retains its OSM way id, highway class, name/ref, original node
coordinates and node identities. Access and direction permissions are derived
conservatively for driving, walking and cycling. Ways with unsupported road
conditions, turn restrictions or restricted/barrier nodes remain visible but
are excluded from local routing. The snapshot does not contain live traffic.
No raster tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org are bulk downloaded or prefetched.
The public dataset is bundled with the app and does not depend on user location.
